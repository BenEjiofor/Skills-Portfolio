4. Reflection on Professional Conduct

Professional conduct is essential both as a student and in any future career, especially in the tech industry. Through this module and my experiences, I’ve learned that professionalism means more than just showing up—it includes acting ethically, being accountable, and respecting others’ work.

From the NTU Student Code and BCS Code of Conduct, I understand the importance of integrity, data protection, and treating others with fairness and respect. During this module, I’ve shown professionalism by managing my time across university, a part-time job, and an independent app project. I’ve avoided plagiarism, met deadlines, and worked independently on tasks.

In my future career, whether working in a tech firm or running my own business, I will carry forward values like clear communication, respect for users' data, inclusivity, and transparency. One area I want to keep improving is documentation and technical clarity, especially when explaining ideas to non-technical audiences.
